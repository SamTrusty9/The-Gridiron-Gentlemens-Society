    League Rules & Format

    The rulebook — refer here any time there's a question about format, waivers, trades, or payouts.

    Season Structure & Schedule

    14-week regular season, followed by a 3-week playoff bracket (Weeks 15–17). Every manager plays exactly 14 regular-season games: 2 vs. their rival, 2 each vs. their other 2 division-mates, and 1 each vs. the 8 managers outside their division. Home/away is balanced league-wide — every manager gets exactly 7 home and 7 away games.

    Divisions & Rivalries

    Six locked-in rivalries. Divisions are built from two rivalry pairs each, so your rival is always a division-mate. Divisions are currently randomized (grouped only to keep rivalries intra-division).
